## Source review

NASA fact pages for Mercury, Venus, Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and
Neptune were retrieved during implementation. Existing Earth/Moon references
remain. Voyager baseline and Proxima references were also checked. Values such
as mean diameters, gravity and Proxima's approximate size are rounded model
quantities, rather than claims of calibrated live measurements.

Sagittarius A* constants were checked against
[NASA's mass and distance reference](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/supermassive-black-hole-sagittarius/)
and [Chandra's J2000 coordinates](https://chandra.si.edu/photo/2026/sgrawind/index.html).
The ring is expressly illustrative; optical size does not represent scientific
observer distance. Spin and gravitational trajectory integration are omitted.

## Limitations

Chromium uses software WebGL (SwiftShader) in this environment. Functional
and visual evidence does not establish physical-device GPU frame rates or
frame pacing. Existing textures/procedural materials and the simple bounded
black-hole observation are intentional pending the later visual tickets.
Vite reports the existing JavaScript chunk over 500 kB; no unrelated performance
refactor is included. No deployment, push, or DNS change is part of this ticket.
