# EH-04 acceptance evidence

Implemented 2026-11-14. Scope: Voyager 1 and Proxima Centauri encounters, the
long Neptune→Voyager→Proxima→Sagittarius A* transit, and optional synchronized
audio. Route, timing and science are unchanged; EH-05 owns the black-hole
arrival/descent and EH-06 owns motion-mode policy and load fallbacks.

## Limitations

- Software WebGL (SwiftShader) only: no real-GPU frame pacing, and audio output was never heard — tests check targets, context state and absence of errors, not the sound itself.
- Mobile Voyager/Proxima inspection and the mobile real-time flight were checked by test assertions; only desktop frames were reviewed by eye.
- Voyager is illustrative: boom lengths are shortened, materials are plausible rather than measured, and the model is the only source (no NASA mesh).
- Sagittarius A* arrival look (EH-05) and reduced-motion handling of streaks/audio intensity (EH-06) are not covered; EH-07 verifies the combined final transit.
