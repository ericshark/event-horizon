Distance, time, and what 1c means

Direct Earth distance is straight-line displacement. Tour distance is sampled along a curved route through the stops; it is longer because of detours. Light-equivalent time is tour distance ÷ 299,792.458 km/s. A massive spacecraft cannot reach light speed. Parker times assume its record speed is sustained indefinitely, without acceleration, braking or fuel limits. Displayed values are rounded; they are not mission forecasts or a traveller’s proper time.

Gaia BH1, tides, and the interior

We use 9.62 solar masses and a non-rotating Schwarzschild model. The horizon radius is about 28.4 km. The tidal display is the Newtonian estimate 2GMh/r³ across h = 2 metres, in m/s²; it is educational, not a biological survival prediction or an exact relativistic calculation. The camera continues after destructive tides as an imaginary observer. A dormant black hole has no luminous accretion disk. The companion and star field are illustrative. Inside, no signal or physical return to Earth is possible; our final overlay is a narrative interface, not transmitted telemetry.
